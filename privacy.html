How We Use Enquiry Information

This notice explains how S&K Wood Floor Specialists uses information submitted through this website.

Information collected

Enquiry forms may collect your name, phone number, email address, postcode, property details, project measurements and information about the flooring work required.

How it is used

We use the information to assess your enquiry, contact you, arrange a viewing, prepare estimates or quotations and manage requested flooring work.

Form processing

The website currently uses FormSubmit to deliver enquiry form information by email. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through the form.

Retention

Enquiry and quotation records may be retained where reasonably necessary for customer service, accounting, insurance or legal purposes.

Your choices

You may ask what information we hold about your enquiry or request correction or deletion where applicable by emailing info@skwoodfloorspecialists.co.uk.
