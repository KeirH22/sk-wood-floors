Why Patterned Floors Need Specialist Care

Sanding across changing grain directions, repairing individual blocks and maintaining pattern alignment require careful planning. Heavy or uneven sanding can flatten edges and distort the pattern.

  • Assess block stability before sanding
  • Use a suitable multi-directional sanding sequence
  • Prepare edges and corners consistently
  • Select a finish that complements the timber
