Your fitting
questions, answered.

Need help choosing between restoring and replacing? We can assess both options.

Explore floor sanding ↗
Can you fit flooring I have already bought?

Send us the product specification or a link. We will check its suitability for the room, base and proposed fitting method before confirming the installation.

Will my subfloor need preparation?

It depends on its flatness, stability and moisture conditions. We assess these first and identify any preparation in the quotation.

Can you fit over underfloor heating?

Suitability depends on the exact flooring product, heating system and installation method. Send the product and heating details so these can be checked against the manufacturer’s requirements.

Does the flooring need to acclimatise?

Follow the chosen manufacturer’s instructions for storage, room conditions and acclimatisation. We can discuss the requirements before the fitting date.

Can you remove the old floor and finish the edges?

Uplift, waste removal, trims, thresholds and skirting details can be discussed and included in the agreed quotation. Tell us what is needed when you enquire.

Do you also sand and restore wooden floors?

Yes. Floor sanding and restoration remain a core part of our work. If the existing floor is suitable for restoration, we can explain that option alongside a replacement.
