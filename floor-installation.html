Before fitting begins

What We Need to Check

The chosen floor is only one part of a successful installation. The subfloor, moisture conditions, room shape, thresholds and skirting details all need to be considered.

  • Subfloor flatness and stability
  • Moisture testing where appropriate
  • Product acclimatisation requirements
  • Expansion gaps and door thresholds
  • Skirting, trims and transitions
  • Furniture and access arrangements
