Restoration scope

Problems We Assess

Damaged Boards

Loose, split or badly damaged boards can be stabilised or replaced where matching material and access allow.

Failed Finishes

Poor coatings, visible sanding marks and uneven surface preparation can often be corrected by resanding.

Gaps and Movement

Resin filler or timber slivers may be suitable, but the floor construction and expected movement must be considered.

Parquet Blocks

Loose or damaged blocks may require local repair before the full floor is sanded and refinished.

Deep Wear and Scratches

Surface wear can often be removed, although deep staining and historic damage may remain visible.

Builder Sanding Correction

Uneven sanding, swirl marks and poor preparation can be assessed and corrected where enough timber remains.

Honest assessment

Restoration Is Not Always the Right Answer

Some floors are too thin, structurally unsound or contaminated to restore safely. We assess the floor before recommending sanding, local repairs or replacement.

  • Floor construction and remaining timber
  • Loose boards or blocks
  • Moisture or subfloor problems
  • Deep contamination and old adhesives
  • Access, furniture and room use
